I am an Associate Professor of Economics at Fudan University. I received my PhD in Economics from Boston University.

My research uses understudied markets and populations to address broader questions in economics. I study how incentives, social norms, discrimination, and institutions shape individual behavior and market outcomes.

A distinctive feature of my research is sustained collaboration with government agencies and community partners. More recently, my research has expanded to social mobility and social networks.
